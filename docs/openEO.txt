1. Create the account

Go to dataspace.copernicus.eu
Click Login, then Register
Fill in the form and confirm your email

2. Test the openEO login. With your backend venv active:

powershell
cd backend
.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
python -c "import openeo; c = openeo.connect('openeo.dataspace.copernicus.eu').authenticate_oidc(); print(c.describe_account())"

It will print a link and a code. Open the link, log in with your CDSE account, and approve. Then the terminal should print your account info.

After this first login, the token is saved on your laptop. Future runs won't ask again.

3. Check the free credits. On the openEO web editor (openeo.dataspace.copernicus.eu), your account shows a credit balance. Free accounts get monthly credits, which is plenty for this project if we only process small areas.
